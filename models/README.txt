The sample models of Glass Forms.

Every file in this folder is either made from numbers by tools/models_make.py (run it to make them
again) or public domain. Nothing here was downloaded, and nothing here carries anyone else's terms.

  teapot.obj   The Utah teapot, from Martin Newell's 28 Bezier patches (1975). The patch data is public
               domain; the triangles are worked out from it by tools/models_make.py, 40 steps to a
               patch's side. As Newell left it: no bottom, a gap round the lid, and the spout and
               handle pushed into the body.
  owl.ply      A made-up owl with four ragged holes in its base, as a scanner leaves them. Every number
               of it is made by tools/models_make.py.
  goblet.ply   A stemmed glass as a single sheet turned about an axis, open at the mouth and under the
               foot. Made by tools/models_make.py.
  gear.stl     A spur gear with a round bore. Made by tools/models_make.py.
  pawn.glb     A chess pawn standing on a plinth: two closed pieces in two nodes. Made by
               tools/models_make.py.

Any OBJ, STL, PLY, glTF or GLB file of your own can be loaded as well; it does not have to be here.
